# Ferry

> Ferry (operated by ThirdRails Ltd) is a London-based development and data integration platform for the industrial edge. It lets operations, IT and data teams in manufacturing, energy, oil & gas and pharmaceuticals integrate factory-floor and frontline data into their IT systems, and build, deploy and monitor custom edge applications and data pipelines on industrial hardware. Ferry ships as a modular Manufacturing Execution System (MES) with agentic AI, deploying via AWS IoT Greengrass or Azure IoT Edge in the customer's own cloud account. Backed by Seedcamp, Asymmetric Capital Partners and Target Global; SOC 2 Type II certified.
